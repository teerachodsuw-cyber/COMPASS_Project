# Methodology

COMPASS predicts competing-risk, multi-horizon outcomes (mortality, major adverse liver
outcomes, and cardiovascular disease) from longitudinal clinical data in patients with
steatotic liver disease and type 2 diabetes, evaluating three candidate deep-learning
architectures (Dynamic-DeepHit, TRACER, SurvLatent ODE) under a semi-competing-risks
framework.
